Field manual

Survival is arithmetic. Know what each action costs before you commit.

RULESET 1.0

01 / Turn-based combat

Each route starts one infected encounter and grants 2 + route threat initial loot items (subject to bag space). Choose Shoot, Melee, Use item, or Escape. Each valid action gives one response from a living infected; killing it ends the encounter without a counterattack. Failed escapes also trigger a response. Successful escapes end the outing with the bag intact.

Shot hit % = clamp(82 − 12×max(0, distance−2) − 15×contact, 20, 92)

Ranged weapons have 82% accuracy at 2 m; each extra meter costs 12 percentage points. At 0 m, accuracy loses another 15 points. A shot consumes one round whether it hits or misses; a hit deals the weapon's damage and uses 1 durability. Pipe melee is available within 1 m, hits 90% of the time, and loses durability only on a hit. Out of melee reach the infected closes by 1 m; at 0–1 m it attacks. Its damage is 6 + 2×route threat, reduced by 2 per barricade tier (minimum 1). When the round counter advances to a multiple of 3 (first on the second response), an unrattled infected has a 45% chance to screech: it skips damage, and escape remains 10 points harder until you try to escape.

Escape % = clamp(65 + 7×distance − 8×threat − 10×wound − 10×screech, 15, 90)

Threat is route tier (0–3); wound is 1 below 50% health; screech is 1 while rattled. A bandage restores up to 25 health and uses the infected's response. Defeat or abandonment returns half of each bag item, rounded down; defeat leaves you at 25% max health (minimum 1). Remaining loot automatically transfers to the home chest.

02 / Weapons & ammunition tree

  • Pipe: 7 damage, 0 ammo, durability 12 hits. Craft: 2 scrap + 1 wood.
  • Revolver: 12 damage, durability 20 shots. Bench I: 4 scrap + 2 parts; one .38 round per shot.
  • Carbine: 18 damage, durability 24 shots. Bench II: 6 scrap + 4 parts; one 5.56 round per shot.
  • .38 ammunition: craft 4 rounds: 1 scrap + 1 parts. 5.56: craft 3 rounds: 2 scrap + 1 parts.
  • Weapon wear accrues only on successful hits. At 0 durability it is unusable; repair costs 2 scrap + 1 parts for up to 10 durability.

Bench I unlocks revolver and .38. Bench II unlocks carbine and 5.56. The starter Pipe is reliable but makes closing distance dangerous.

03 / Routes & salvage

  • Playground · threat 0: available from day 1; starts at 3 m. Pool: timber, cloth, scrap.
  • Park · threat 1: unlocks day 2; starts at 4 m. Pool: timber, rations, cloth, parts.
  • Gym · threat 2: unlocks day 3; starts at 2 m. Pool: scrap, parts, cloth, rations.
  • City Square · threat 3: unlocks day 4; starts at 5 m. Pool: parts, scrap, rations, .38 rounds, 5.56 rounds.

Each route has one infected and draws 2 + threat items uniformly from its pool, subject to bag space. Clearing it adds 1–2 bonus items: scrap on Playground/Park, parts at the Gym, or .38 rounds at City Square. Infected health is 16–22 + 7×threat; damage is 6 + 2×threat. Escape odds decrease as threat rises. Starting bag capacity is 8; Field Pack adds 4 per tier (up to 20).

04 / Shelter & daily loop

  • Rooftop garden (max 3): +1 ration each night per tier. Tier costs: 3 wood + 1 scrap; 5 wood + 3 scrap; 7 wood + 5 scrap.
  • Workbench (max 2): tier I costs 3 scrap + 2 wood; tier II costs 6 scrap + 2 wood + 2 parts. Unlocks the ranged weapons and ammunition above.
  • Door barricade (max 3): reduce incoming damage by 2 per tier. Costs: 3 wood + 2 scrap; 5 wood + 4 scrap; 7 wood + 6 scrap.
  • Field Pack (max 3): +4 bag slots per tier. Costs: 2 cloth + 2 wood; 4 cloth + 4 wood; 6 cloth + 6 wood.
  • Reinforced bunk (max 3): each tier adds 10 max and current health when installed, then restores 8 health at each dawn. Costs: 3 wood + 2 cloth; 5 wood + 4 cloth; 7 wood + 6 cloth.

One expedition per day. Return, victory, escape, defeat, or abandonment moves you to night; fabrication and upgrades are un-timed and night-only. Ending night consumes 1 ration or costs 12 health if none is available (minimum 1), then applies garden production and restores 8 health per bunk tier. A new day begins with routes unlocked by day number.

Next rations = current rations − 1 (if available) + garden tiers
